📞 Production Telecom Gateway Specifications (PSTN / GSM / IVR)

For production deployment on Indian telecom networks (BSNL, Jio, Airtel) via Exotel, Tata Tele Business Services, or Twilio Voice:

POST /api/telephony/doctor-inbound

Toll-free 1800 incoming call webhook. Detects caller ID, fetches profile, answers call, and streams VoiceXML / TwiML audio.

POST /api/telephony/doctor-turn

Continuous speech stream handler. Ingests raw telecom PCM audio, runs ASR, updates 4-turn state machine, and generates audio response.

POST /api/telephony/weather-missed-call

Missed-call gateway. Rejects incoming call after 1 ring (saving farmer balance) and dispatches Outbound Dialing (OBD) audio bulletin.
